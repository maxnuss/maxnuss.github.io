Flashback Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 7, 2026

Flashback respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

Flashback does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data.

Photos and Device Access

If you grant Flashback access to your photo library, that access is used only to display and shuffle your photos on your device. Your photos are not uploaded to our servers or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Flashback does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or other third-party services that collect user data.

Data Retention

Because Flashback does not collect user data, we do not retain any personal information.

Children’s Privacy

Flashback does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: flashbackappsupport@gmail.com
